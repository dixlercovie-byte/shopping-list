Welcome

Under the guidance of Rabbi Millrod, our shul is a place where tradition meets warmth. Whether you come for daily minyan, a Shabbos meal, a shiur, or simply to say hello, you'll find an open door and a seat at the table.

Everyone is welcome — families, singles, seniors, and visitors. Come as you are.
